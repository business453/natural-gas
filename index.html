Signal guide: price ↑ + OI ↑ = long buildup; price ↑ + OI ↓ = short covering; price ↓ + OI ↑ = short buildup; price ↓ + OI ↓ = long unwinding. Classification compares the first and latest observation inside the selected window and uses minimum-move filters of 0.02% for price and 0.05% for OI to reduce flat-market noise. It is a positioning heuristic, not a standalone buy/sell instruction. History starts when this dashboard connects to the selected contract and is held in memory until the server restarts or another contract is selected.
